How to use this

  1. Keep this open beside the demo environment. Work through the steps in order, clicking exactly what each step says.
  2. Compare the screen to You should see, then mark Pass, Fail or Skip and add a note. Everything autosaves in this browser, and a "Saved" message confirms each change.
  3. Download report (JSON) saves every step with its expected result, If-not hint, your result and notes. Send it to , or upload it to a coding agent as context. It also works as a backup: Load report restores it on any machine.
  4. 📸 Video shots: steps with a blue Capture for the demo video callout are scenes in the recorded demo. Paste a clean screenshot there (no notes or popups showing). Recording mode fades out every other step. When the meter is full, click 🎬 Make the movie. It downloads a bundle that Vaibhav's autonomous-demo-recorder turns into a narrated MP4: unzip it and double-click make-movie.command. ▶ Preview plays a quick narrated slideshow in the browser first.
  5. Screenshots: click a step (or its notes box), then press ⌘V to paste a screenshot. You can also drag an image onto a step, or use Add image. Images are shrunk, saved with your notes, and included in the JSON report.
  6. When you're done, copy the summary at the bottom and send it to .
